Before You Read This…

Just One Thing 🤍

Don’t rush this.

Read it slowly.

Because I didn’t write this casually.

Okay… here it is.

I made this for you because sometimes words get lost in normal conversations. Sometimes messages get buried between tired replies, busy days, and different time zones. But what I feel for you isn’t something I want to leave half-said.

You came into my life unexpectedly, but somehow you stayed in my thoughts intentionally. And that difference matters. Because being curious about someone is easy. But choosing someone, even on their quiet days, even on their low-energy days — that’s different.

I notice the little things about you. The way your tone changes when you're excited. The way you talk when you're tired. The way you still try to show up even when you don’t have much energy left. You probably think it’s small. It’s not small to me.

Distance doesn’t make me doubt this. It doesn’t make it weaker. If anything, it makes it clearer. Because when there’s distance, you don’t stay because it’s convenient. You stay because you actually want to. And I want to.

I don’t just like the fun parts of you. I like the calm parts. The tired parts. The slightly moody parts. The quiet parts. The soft parts you don’t always show loudly.

And I also want to say something honestly. There was a moment when I pulled away. When I acted distant because I felt pushed aside. You were busy, you were snowboarding, you were living your day — and instead of telling you how I felt, I chose silence.

That wasn’t because I didn’t care. It was actually because I cared too much. And I didn’t know how to say, “Hey… I miss you.” Without sounding needy.

If that made you feel sad, I’m sorry. Not in a dramatic way. Just in a real way. You didn’t deserve to feel like I disappeared on you.

I’m still learning how to handle my feelings without shutting down. But one thing hasn’t changed — I never stopped choosing you. Even in silence. Even when I acted quiet.

If I were next to you right now, I wouldn’t try to impress you. I wouldn’t rush anything. I’d just sit close. Let my hand rest against yours. Maybe pull you in slowly and just exist there for a moment. No performance. No pressure. Just us.

Out of all the randomness in this world, out of all the conversations that could’ve meant nothing, I’m glad ours didn’t.

And I’m not here because it’s exciting. I’m here because it feels right.

I still choose you. Every time. ❤️
