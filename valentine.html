Happy Valentine’s Day, my favorite person.

Even though we’re miles apart, I never feel like you’re truly far from me. Every conversation, every random moment, every time we laugh or even miss each other reminds me how lucky I am to have you.

Distance isn’t always easy. There are tired days, busy days, and moments when we both just want to be in the same place. But somehow, even through all of that, we still choose each other. And that means everything to me.

I love how we can talk about anything — from the most random topics to the deeper things we don’t tell everyone. I love that even when you’re exhausted, you still make time for me.

Sometimes I wish I could just teleport to you. Not for anything dramatic. Just to sit next to you. To hold you. To feel your warmth without a screen between us.

I don’t just look forward to the exciting moments with you. I look forward to the quiet ones too. The simple ones. The ones where we’re just existing together.

No matter how many kilometers are between us right now, I’m still choosing you. And I can’t wait for the day distance isn’t something we even have to think about anymore.

I’m really grateful you’re mine. ❤️
