14 February • Just For You

Happy Valentine’s Day 🤍

I made this just for you. Not because I had to. But because you matter to me more than you probably realize.

From the moment we started talking, something felt different. Even when you're tired, even when you're quiet, even when the day feels heavy — I still choose you.

I love the way you tell stories. I love the way your voice changes when you're excited. I love that even on your tired days, you still say you like spending time with me.

Distance doesn’t scare me. Time zones don’t scare me. Because what we have isn’t about being close physically — it’s about choosing each other again and again.

If I could be there right now, I’d just sit next to you, hold your hand gently, and let the world slow down for a minute.

I always will. ❤️
